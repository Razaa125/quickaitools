How to Upscale Images Free Without Losing Quality

📅 January 2026⏱ 5 min read

Whether you have an old photo you want to restore or a low-resolution image you need to print large, AI upscaling tools can dramatically improve image quality without the blurring you get from traditional resizing methods. Here is how to do it for free.

What is AI Image Upscaling?

Traditional image resizing simply stretches pixels, causing blurring and pixelation. AI upscaling uses machine learning models trained on millions of images to intelligently fill in the missing detail. The result is a sharper, cleaner image that looks like it was always higher resolution.

Best Free AI Upscaling Tools

iLoveIMG offers free 2x upscaling for any image type and is the easiest to use with no signup required. Upscale.media provides up to 4x upscaling with good results for photos and portraits. Waifu2x is specifically designed for anime, illustrations and artwork and produces outstanding results for those image types. Let's Enhance is best for product photos and real-world photography.

How to Get the Best Results

  • Start with the highest resolution version of your image that you have
  • Use 2x upscaling for most cases — 4x can sometimes look over-sharpened
  • Enable noise reduction if the original image is grainy or compressed
  • Save the output as PNG for the best quality without compression artifacts
  • For old photos, try a tool with face enhancement to restore facial details
